Order update texts

Effective November 9, 2026

Bubble House LLC, 1103 US Hwy 1, Sebastian, FL 32958, sends text messages about customers' orders to people who choose to get them in the Bubble House ordering app for iPhone. The app is not public yet. This page shows the sign-up screen exactly as customers will see it, so anyone can check how consent is collected.

How customers opt in

  • When they create an account in the app, customers enter their name, mobile number and email address.
  • Below those fields is a box labeled "Text me order updates". It is unchecked by default. Customers who want texts check it themselves.
  • Texts are optional. Customers can create an account and order without checking the box, and the app shows the order status either way.
  • Customers can turn order texts on or off later on the app's Account screen, which shows the same wording.
  • We record the time, the wording shown, where the customer agreed and the phone number for every choice.

What we send

Only texts about the customer's own orders: when an order is received (with its ready time), when it is ready for pickup, and if it is delayed, canceled or refunded. Usually 2 texts per order. No marketing texts are sent under this sign-up; marketing would need its own, separate opt-in.

Sample messages

  • First text after opting in: Bubble House order updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
  • Bubble House: we got order #1042. It will be ready about 2:45 PM. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Bubble House: order #1042 is ready for pickup at 1103 US Hwy 1, Sebastian. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Bubble House: sorry, order #1042 is running late. New ready time about 3:05 PM. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Owner alert: Bubble House alert: order #1042 hasn't reached the kitchen screen. Open Check now on the owner page. Reply STOP to opt out.

Owner alerts

The owners of Bubble House also get texts from the same number about orders and kitchen problems that need attention, such as a paid order that hasn't reached the kitchen screen. Each owner opts in on the restaurant's owner settings page, which only the owners can open: they enter their own mobile number and check a box that starts unchecked. HELP and STOP work the same way.

Help and opting out

Reply STOP to any text to stop order texts; we confirm once and send nothing more. Reply START to turn them back on. Reply HELP, or email contact@ilovebubblehouse.com, for help. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We will not share your mobile phone number or your consent to receive text messages with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of use.
