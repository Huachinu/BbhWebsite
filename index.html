The self-serve ramen station

Our automated Neo Cucina machines cook your ramen for you, right in the shop.

  1. Pick your ramen and toppingsChoose a pack from the shelves of ramen brands on the wall, and any toppings you like.
  2. Pay at the counterBring your picks to the counter and pay.
  3. Scan the barcodeScan your ramen pack at any Neo Cucina machine.
  4. Let the machine cook itIt cooks your ramen while you wait.
